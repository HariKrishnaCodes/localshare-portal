Delete Your Account

Users can request deletion of their account and associated data by submitting the request below.

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy standards, you do not need to have the mobile app installed or be logged in to submit this deletion request. Once submitted, our team will verify your identity and process the removal of your personal information.

Important Notice Before Requesting Deletion

Submitting this request initiates the account and data deletion verification process. Submitting this form sends a deletion request for processing. Once an account is deleted, some information may not be recoverable.

Transparency & Process

What Happens After You Request Deletion?

Here is the step-by-step workflow followed by [COMPANY NAME] to handle account and data deletion requests.

1

We Verify the Request

To prevent fraudulent, unauthorized, or malicious deletions, our team verifies that the requester is the authorized owner of the specified account (e.g., via a verification email sent to the registered address).

2

We Process the Request

Once verified, our administrators initiate the formal account decommission workflow across our active databases, services, and authentication providers.

3

Data Deleted or Anonymized

Account-related data is deleted or anonymized according to our data retention requirements. Profile records, credentials, personal preferences, and identifiers are purged from primary systems.

4

Legal Compliance Retention

Certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security/legal purposes (such as financial records, tax compliance, dispute records, or fraud prevention logs).

Summary of Data Types Handled During Deletion

Permanently Deleted or Anonymized

  • User profile details (Full Name, Display Name, Bio)
  • Email address and login credentials / tokens
  • In-app personalized configurations and preferences
  • Device tokens and push notification IDs
  • User-uploaded content or stored media files

Retained Where Legally Necessary

  • Transaction receipts and invoice identifiers (tax & commercial recordkeeping laws)
  • Security incident logs and anti-fraud telemetry (isolated access)
  • Records of deletion request fulfillment for legal audit defense
  • Encrypted routine disaster recovery backups (purged in cycle)
Notice Regarding Recovery: Once an account is deleted, some information may not be recoverable. If you wish to use LocalShare again in the future, you will need to register a brand new account.
