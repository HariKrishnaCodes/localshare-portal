1. Introduction

Thank you for choosing LocalShare ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

When you download, install, access, or use our mobile application, or visit our support website at https://localshare.infotechs.co.in, you trust us with your personal data. We take that trust seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, handle, and disclose your data, as well as your statutory rights regarding that data under applicable global privacy legislation.

If you do not agree with the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of LocalShare and uninstall the mobile application from your device.

Depending on how you interact with the application, we may collect:

  • Information you provide directly: Data submitted when registering an account, completing your user profile, contacting customer care, or requesting account assistance.
  • Information collected automatically: Technical identifiers and device usage telemetry captured automatically when the application runs on your mobile operating system.

3. Account Information

To access personalized services within LocalShare, you may be required to register a user account. In doing so, we may collect:

  • Your full name or chosen display alias
  • Your email address
  • A secure hashed password or authentication authorization tokens
  • Account identifier number or username
  • Optional profile preferences or avatar imagery uploaded by you

You may review, update, or correct your account details at any time through the in-app account settings menu or by contacting support at support@localshare.infotechs.co.in.

4. Device / Technical Information

When you use our mobile application, our systems may automatically record technical parameters to ensure app stability and optimal display formatting, including:

  • Device Hardware: Device model, manufacturer, hardware specifications, and screen resolution.
  • Operating System: Android OS version, security patch level, and system language settings.
  • Network & Connectivity: IP address, mobile network status, and connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • Crash Diagnostics: Anonymous stack traces and crash dump files to help our development team identify and resolve application errors.

We do not track continuous physical GPS location in the background unless explicitly required by an in-app feature and authorized by you via the Android operating system runtime permission dialogue.

5. How We Use Information

We process your personal information for lawful and legitimate purposes, including:

  • To create, authenticate, and manage your user account.
  • To provide the core features and functionality of LocalShare.
  • To respond to user support inquiries, bug reports, and technical assistance requests.
  • To notify you about critical security alerts, software updates, and policy revisions.
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraudulent usage, security breaches, or terms violations.
  • To comply with legal obligations, regulatory disclosures, or enforceable law enforcement requests.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers.

We may only share information in the following limited situations:

  • With Authorized Service Providers: Trusted infrastructure and hosting partners who operate on our behalf under strict data processing agreements and confidentiality obligations.
  • For Legal & Compliance Reasons: If required by court order, applicable statute, or regulatory requirement.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with or during negotiations of any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business by another company.
  • With Your Consent: We may disclose your personal information for any other purpose with your explicit prior consent.

7. Data Security

We implement comprehensive technical, administrative, and physical security measures designed to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

  • All network transmissions between your device and our endpoints are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3).
  • Sensitive stored credentials are salted and hashed using industry-recommended cryptographic standards.
  • Access to internal database records is strictly restricted to authorized engineering personnel on a need-to-know basis with multi-factor authentication.

While we strive to employ commercial safeguards to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% immune from vulnerability.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered, including:

  • Maintaining your active account on LocalShare.
  • Providing you with ongoing customer service and product features.
  • Complying with applicable legal, accounting, tax, or reporting requirements.

When personal data is no longer required, it is either securely deleted from our production systems or irreversibly aggregated and anonymized so that it can no longer be associated with you.

9. Account Deletion & Data Erasure

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy requirements and data protection laws (such as GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

You do not need to be currently logged into the app or have the app installed to submit a deletion request.

How to request deletion:

  • Visit our dedicated, publicly accessible web portal: Request Account Deletion
  • Or email our privacy desk directly at: support@localshare.infotechs.co.in

Upon receiving your request, we verify your authorization, initiate data decommissioning, and purge or anonymize your personal records. Note that certain minimal transactional or security audit logs may be retained where strictly required by governing law. Once an account is deleted, some information may not be recoverable.

10. Children's Privacy

Our application and services are not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal identifiable information from minors.

If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately at support@localshare.infotechs.co.in. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected data from a child, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our active databases.

12. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and applicable data protection legislation, you may hold the following statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: The right to request copies of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: The right to request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): The right to request that we delete your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: The right to restrict or object to how we process your information.
  • Right to Data Portability: The right to request transfer of your data to another organization or directly to you in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please reach out to our privacy representative at support@localshare.infotechs.co.in.
